\section{Retaining the highest-visit domain labels}\label{app:adulthead}
Table~\ref{tab:adulthead} repeats the count-information exercise after
retaining both observed classification labels for the highest-visit domains.
For the remaining domains it retains only the numbers positive under
Piedomains alone and under the provider alone. The ordering uses visit count
with domain name to break ties. The count range is sharp under exactly this
information: the head contribution is fixed, while the tail discrepancies
can occupy any compatible tail domains. At zero retained domains it equals
the range in Table~\ref{tab:adult}. The provider's observed visit share lies
inside every range.
